The books

The per-lecture References sections cite the works below by author and short title, with pages. Where a work’s conventions differ from this course’s (an inner product linear in the first slot, ℏ=1\hbar=1ℏ=1, ω\omegaω for a frequency), the lectures translate at the point of use (see the Notation Bible).
